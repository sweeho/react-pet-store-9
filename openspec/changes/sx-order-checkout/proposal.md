## Why

Recover the order-checkout behaviour of the legacy Pet Store: order and card persistence, checkout validation, order numbering, asynchronous submission to order processing, address handling.

## What Changes

- Adds the order-checkout capability spec (38 requirements, of which 27 are approved and carry keys).

## Impact

Affects order, card, address, line item and identifier-counter data, and the hand-off to order processing.
